\opening{Dear Editor-in-Chief and Associate Editors,}

We submit the critical survey

\begin{center}
\textbf{Graph-Based and Self-Supervised Knowledge Tracing in Sparse-Concept Settings: A Critical Survey}
\end{center}

for consideration as a \textbf{Survey and Tutorial} article in \textit{IEEE Transactions on Learning Technologies}. The compiled manuscript is 14 double-column pages (TLT Survey and Tutorial cap: 18 pages, effective 1 January 2025). We submit through the IEEE TLT Author Portal (IEEE account), not ScholarOne.

\textbf{Fit to TLT.} Knowledge tracing is the mastery-estimation engine of intelligent tutoring and adaptive platforms. This PRISMA 2020-informed critical survey synthesizes how Graph-Based and Self-Supervised KT methods are constructed and evaluated under sparse-concept conditions, and it translates coded gaps into an operational GO/NO-GO checklist for three tutoring jobs (new-KC onboarding, treating a graph as a skill map, and acting on $P(r{=}1)$). The paper is intended to meet TLT's dual bar: a computer-science taxonomy and protocol audit, plus an explicit learning-technology use case that a learning engineer can apply without treating the survey as a classroom result.

\textbf{Relation to Shen et al.\ (TLT 2024).} Shen et al.\ remain the broad TLT map of KT models, variants, and applications. This paper does not replace that survey and does not claim to be the first KT review. The operational question is narrower and complementary: under which \emph{coded} evaluation conditions may Graph-KT or SSL-KT inform tutoring when KCs are sparse or newly introduced? That question is answered with (i)~graph provenance $\times$ encoder coding, (ii)~locked SSL pretext families ($N_S=9$), (iii)~separation of interaction sparsity from item, learner, and strict KC cold-start, (iv)~an ECE reporting audit ($0/37$), and (v)~a three-decision GO/NO-GO checklist plus Table~VI for ITS / adaptive / formative use. Shen does not perform that sparse-KC / calibration / deployment audit.

\textbf{Reliability (honest).} The 20-paper coding sample was double-coded twice. Locator-assisted pass (\texttt{NguyenKT}): $158/160$, mean $\kappa=0.98$. Independent pass (\texttt{DaoMT}, no locator): $157/160$, mean $\kappa=0.97$. The two coefficients are not pooled. Screening and full-text second-screener pair files are still absent. Count-based findings are protocol-coded audit results.

\textbf{Locked corpus facts (not a new count in this letter):}
\begin{itemize}
    \item PRISMA flow: 2,017 identified records from six core databases plus arXiv preprint monitoring; 37 primary-core studies.
    \item $N_G=36$, $N_S=9$, $N_{G\cap S}=8$.
    \item Strict zero-exposure KC cold-start: $0/37$. Item cold-start: $1/37$ (SINKT question hold-out). Learner cold-start: $0/37$.
    \item ECE reported in the published manuscripts: $0/37$.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{What this paper does not claim.} We do not report a locked leakage percentage, a cross-paper AUC league table, a reproduced representation-collapse statistic, or an executed \emph{pyKT-ColdStart} benchmark. The last item is an agenda specification only.
