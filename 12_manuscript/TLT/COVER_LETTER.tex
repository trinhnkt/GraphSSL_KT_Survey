\opening{Dear Editor-in-Chief and Associate Editors,}

We submit the critical survey

\begin{center}
\textbf{Graph-Based and Self-Supervised Knowledge Tracing in Sparse-Concept Settings: A Critical Survey}
\end{center}

for consideration as a \textbf{Survey and Tutorial} article in \textit{IEEE Transactions on Learning Technologies}. The compiled anonymous manuscript is 18 double-column pages; the author-identified version is 18 pages (TLT Survey and Tutorial cap: 18 pages, effective 1 January 2025). We submit through the IEEE TLT Author Portal (IEEE account), not ScholarOne.

\textbf{Fit to TLT (dual bar).} Knowledge tracing is the mastery-estimation engine of intelligent tutoring and adaptive platforms. This critical survey of 44 primary-core PDFs meets TLT's dual requirement: (i)~a computer-science coding audit of Graph-Based and Self-Supervised KT under sparse-concept conditions, and (ii)~an explicit learning-technology use case---a three-job go/not-enough-evidence checklist (new-KC onboarding, treating a graph as a skill map, acting on $P(r{=}1)$) that a learning engineer can apply without reading the survey as a classroom result.

\textbf{Why this paper after Shen et al.\ (TLT 2024).} Shen et al.\ remain the broad TLT map of KT models, variants, and applications. This submission does not replace that survey and does not claim primacy. A TLT reader who already knows Shen still needs answers Shen does not code: when may Graph/SSL-KT support sparse or newly introduced KCs? We answer with (i)~graph provenance $\times$ encoder coding ($N_G=40$), (ii)~SSL pretext and view-source coding ($N_S=16$; $N_{G\cap S}=12$; not a saturated taxonomy), (iii)~interaction sparsity versus item / learner / strict KC cold-start, (iv)~manuscript ECE reporting $0/44$, and (v)~the learning-technology decision table plus the T1--T3 checklist. Those five deliverables are the incremental contribution relative to Shen.

\textbf{Reliability (honest).} The 20-paper coding sample was double-coded twice. After excluding three invariant all-NO fields for which Cohen's $\kappa$ is undefined ($p_e=1$), the locator-assisted pass (\texttt{NguyenKT}) yields mean $\kappa=0.97$ over five defined fields ($158/160$ cells). The independent pass (\texttt{DaoMT}, no locator) yields mean $\kappa=0.96$ over the same five defined fields ($157/160$). The two coefficients are not pooled. Screening agreement is available only for a deposited independently double-screened subset (11 title/abstract pairs, raw agreement $10/11$; 1 full-text pair, raw agreement $0/1$; $\kappa$ undefined at both stages). Identification $N_{\mathrm{rec}}=2{,}017$ is from the locked search log; stage counts 750/140/103 are from the search-expansion report and are not backed by a deposited record-level ledger. The 44 primary-core PDFs were identity-checked by the authors. Screening and primary coding were performed by an AI research assistant; human coding exists for the 20-paper sample, so the coding coefficients measure agreement between human coders and an AI-generated, partly adjudicated reference. A post hoc venue probe found ten further Graph/SSL-KT candidates outside the coded core; they are reported, not coded. Count-based findings are protocol-coded audit results.

\textbf{Locked corpus facts:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Search-log identification $N_{\mathrm{rec}}=2{,}017$ from six core databases plus arXiv preprint monitoring. Stage counts in the PRISMA-style figure are original-path report arithmetic ($140-103=37$); a record-level screening ledger is not deposited.     \item $N_G=40$, $N_S=16$, $N_{G\cap S}=12$; provenance display $24/7/9$.

\end{itemize}

\textbf{What this paper does not claim.} We do not report a locked leakage percentage, a cross-paper AUC/ECE league table, a reproduced representation-collapse statistic, a classroom deployment study, or an executed \emph{pyKT-ColdStart} benchmark. The last item is an agenda specification only. Classroom outcomes are out of Survey and Tutorial scope for this manuscript.
