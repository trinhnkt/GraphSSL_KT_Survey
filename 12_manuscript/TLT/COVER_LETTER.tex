\opening{Dear Editor-in-Chief and Associate Editors,}

We submit the critical survey

\begin{center}
\textbf{Graph-Based and Self-Supervised Knowledge Tracing in Sparse-Concept Settings: A Critical Survey}
\end{center}

for consideration as a \textbf{Survey and Tutorial} article in \textit{IEEE Transactions on Learning Technologies}. The compiled manuscript is 14 double-column pages (TLT Survey and Tutorial cap: 18 pages, effective 1 January 2025). We submit through the IEEE TLT Author Portal (IEEE account), not ScholarOne.

\textbf{Fit to TLT (dual bar).} Knowledge tracing is the mastery-estimation engine of intelligent tutoring and adaptive platforms. This PRISMA 2020-informed critical survey meets TLT's dual requirement: (i)~a computer-science taxonomy and protocol audit of Graph-Based and Self-Supervised KT under sparse-concept conditions, and (ii)~an explicit learning-technology use case---a three-job GO/NO-GO checklist (new-KC onboarding, treating a graph as a skill map, acting on $P(r{=}1)$) that a learning engineer can apply without reading the survey as a classroom result.

\textbf{Why this paper after Shen et al.\ (TLT 2024).} Shen et al.\ remain the broad TLT map of KT models, variants, and applications. This submission does not replace that survey and does not claim primacy. A TLT reader who already knows Shen still needs answers Shen does not code: when may Graph/SSL-KT support sparse or newly introduced KCs? We answer with (i)~graph provenance $\times$ encoder coding ($N_G=36$), (ii)~locked SSL pretext families ($N_S=9$), (iii)~interaction sparsity versus item / learner / strict KC cold-start, (iv)~manuscript ECE reporting $0/37$ plus one survey-computed \texttt{REPRODUCED} ECE overlay (DyGKT/ASSIST17), and (v)~Table~VI plus the T1--T3 checklist. Those five deliverables are the incremental contribution relative to Shen.

\textbf{Reliability (honest).} The 20-paper coding sample was double-coded twice. After excluding three invariant all-NO fields for which Cohen's $\kappa$ is undefined ($p_e=1$), the locator-assisted pass (\texttt{NguyenKT}) yields mean $\kappa=0.97$ over five defined fields ($158/160$ cells). The independent pass (\texttt{DaoMT}, no locator) yields mean $\kappa=0.96$ over the same five defined fields ($157/160$). The two coefficients are not pooled. Screening and full-text second-screener pair files remain absent; title/abstract and full-text stages used a primary screener with team consensus on borderline cases. Count-based findings are protocol-coded audit results.

\textbf{Locked corpus facts (not a new count in this letter):}
\begin{itemize}
    \item PRISMA flow: 2,017 identified records from six core databases plus arXiv preprint monitoring; 37 primary-core studies.
    \item $N_G=36$, $N_S=9$, $N_{G\cap S}=8$.
    \item Strict zero-exposure KC cold-start: $0/37$. Item cold-start: $1/37$ (SINKT question hold-out). Learner cold-start: $0/37$.
    \item ECE reported in the published manuscripts: $0/37$.
    \item One survey-computed \texttt{REPRODUCED} ECE overlay (DyGKT / ASSIST17; equal-width $M=10$; ECE~$=0.0361$; Brier~$=0.1770$) is reported in the paper; it does not alter the $0/37$ manuscript-reporting count.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{What this paper does not claim.} We do not report a locked leakage percentage, a cross-paper AUC/ECE league table, a reproduced representation-collapse statistic, a classroom deployment study, or an executed \emph{pyKT-ColdStart} benchmark. The last item is an agenda specification only. The DyGKT ECE figure is a single-run author-code overlay, not a tutoring deployment result. Classroom outcomes are out of Survey and Tutorial scope for this manuscript.
