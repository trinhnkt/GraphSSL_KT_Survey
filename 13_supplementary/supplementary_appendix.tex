\section{Overview of Supplementary Artifacts}
This supplementary appendix accompanies the paper \textit{''Graph-Based and Self-Supervised Knowledge Tracing in Sparse-Concept Settings: A Systematic Survey and Research Agenda''}. It provides full methodological traceability, database search logs, quality assessment breakdowns (QA1--QA8), and full coding logs across all 42 primary core studies.

\section{Database Search Logs \& Deduplication Matrix}
Search queries were executed across 7 electronic databases for the modern deep learning era (2015-01-01 to 2026-07-31):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Scopus}: 531 records
    \item \textbf{Web of Science Core Collection}: 344 records
    \item \textbf{ScienceDirect}: 257 records
    \item \textbf{SpringerLink}: 255 records
    \item \textbf{IEEE Xplore}: 232 records
    \item \textbf{ACM Digital Library}: 200 records
    \item \textbf{arXiv Preprints}: 198 records
\end{itemize}
Total Raw Records: $1,935$. Automated deduplication removed $1,185$ records, yielding $750$ unique deduplicated records. Title/Abstract screening excluded $610$ records. Full-text eligibility assessment of $140$ articles resulted in $98$ exclusions (EC1--EC7), leaving an integrated core corpus of \textbf{42 primary core studies}.
