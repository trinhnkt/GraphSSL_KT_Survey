## A1-C07 CoSKT

Chunyun Zhang, Hebo Ma, Chaoran Cui, Yumo Yao, Weiran Xu, Yunfeng Zhang, and Yuling Ma. IEEE Transactions on Learning Technologies, vol. 17, pp. 1476–1488, 2024. DOI 10.1109/TLT.2024.3386750. IEEE document 10495188. Crossref gives the year only. The year 2024 is inside the search window.

The authors’ abstract, retrieved from OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar on 2026-11-22, is a knowledge-tracing abstract: it predicts the response to a target exercise from learning sequences. It describes an auxiliary consistency objective between a common knowledge state, learned from similar students, and the individual knowledge state. That description is the kind of objective the locked S-criterion covers. The abstract does not give the loss equation, so S is not finally coded. The abstract describes overlap of learning experiences, attention, and a gate. It does not describe an explicit graph. G is not coded from that silence.
