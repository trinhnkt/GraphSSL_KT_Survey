5  Conclusion
In this paper, we proposed the SGKT model, an innovative fusion of subject knowl-
edge mapping that enriches the dynamics among knowledge concepts while also tak-
ing into account the complexity and interrelations of problems for an intricate scru-
tiny of student learning interactions. By weaving together knowledge concepts and 
question attributes within a graph-based knowledge tracing architecture, the SGKT 
model not only amplifies prediction accuracy and bolsters interpretability but also 
distinctly outperforms established methods, as evidenced through rigorous validation 
using the real-world ASSISTments dataset. This method not only yields deep insights 
into students’ knowledge states, but also serves as an efficacious instrument for 
detecting learning hurdles and crafting tailored intervention strategies. The deploy-
ment of the SGKT model illuminates its vast potential within the educational tech-
nology sphere, heralding its positive implications for curriculum design and resource 
distribution. The ultimate goal is to provide every student with a personalized learn-
ing journey, thus enhancing learning efficacy and fostering educational fairness.
