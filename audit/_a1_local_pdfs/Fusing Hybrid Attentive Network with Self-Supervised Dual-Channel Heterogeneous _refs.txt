However, there are still some limitations in our method. On one
hand, the proposed graph network cannot effectively construct the
representation of the newly updated questions in online systems. On
the other hand, we capture a student’s long-term and short-term intents
in historical sequences by the proposed hybrid attention network, but
ignore the differences of initial knowledge states of students. In the
future, we plan to introduce a meta-learning paradigm to address the
above limitations in the KT field, and exploit meta-learning at the KT
model level and HIN at the data level simultaneously.
