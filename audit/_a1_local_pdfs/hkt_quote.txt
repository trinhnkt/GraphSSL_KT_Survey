• DTransformer: Based on the Diagnostic Transformer, DTransformer (Yin et al., 2023) builds a hierarchical framework from the 
question level to the knowledge level, explicitly diagnosing learners’ knowledge proficiency for each question and incorporates 
contrastive learning to enhance performance.
• StableKT: Based on a linear-biased attention mechanism, StableKT (Li, Bai, Guo, Liu et al., 2024) addresses the sequence 
length generalization problem through distance-proportional attention penalties, effectively maintaining stable predictions 
across interaction sequences of varying lengths while enhancing performance.
