modeling flexibility, data adaptability, and educational utility.
To this end, we proposed four forward-looking research directions: (i) automatic discovery
and completion of graph structures; (ii) leveraging large-scale models to enhance the modeling
capacity and generalization performance of GbKT models; (iii) constructing multimodal graph
structures to incorporate diverse learning signals; and (iv) improving model interpretability by
integrating educational psychology and cognitive science theories.
These directions are expected to drive the development of GbKT models toward greater
intelligence, precision, and usability, expanding their application potential and research value in
intelligent education systems.
