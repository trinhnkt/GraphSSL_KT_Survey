texts are still difficult to use. 2) When the expert’s pre-
definition is not used, automatic modeling of the complex
knowledge structure of the knowledge capsule at same layer
is insufficient, and the knowledge capsules graph network
needs to be improved to better participate in the routing
calculation. 3) Our model does not consider the important
impact of forgetting behavior and contextual time interval
on the prediction task and interpretability, and cannot
evaluate the evolution of knowledge based on the time
interval. 4) Need to optimize the routing strategy to reduce
the amount of parameters and computational overhead.
