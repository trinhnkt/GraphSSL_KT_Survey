6 Conclusions
This paper presents a cross-domain knowledge tracing model based on transfer learning
called AEGOT-CDKT, aiming to bridge the knowledge gap between different domains while
optimizing and enhancing the performance of knowledge tracing in new domains. The core
innovation of AEGOT-CDKT lies in introducing an alignment module capable of handling
and reducing the distributional differences of learners’ knowledge states across different
domains. Moreover, it applies Graph Optimal Transport algorithm to effectively align the
graph structures of different domains, thus enhancing the complementarity of student knowl-
edge state representations and providing learners with more precise learning guidance.
Finally, experimental studies and visual analyses are conducted to validate the models
excellent performance in various target domains, further demonstrating the crucial role of
transfer learning in improving the generalization and adaptability of knowledge tracing mod-
els.
