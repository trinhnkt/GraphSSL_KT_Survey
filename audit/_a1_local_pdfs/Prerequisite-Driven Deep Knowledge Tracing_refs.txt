For the future work, we would like to explore other ways
to better express those concept interdependencies, for exam-
ple, graph embedding. In addition, we also would like to
jointly optimize the prerequisite relation extraction process
and knowledge tracing process to better identify prerequisite
relations and estimate students’ knowledge states.
