# H3 — Novelty positioning

This survey is **not** claimed as the first KT or Graph-KT review.

| Prior work | What it is | What it does not do (relative to this survey) |
|---|---|---|
| Abdelrahman et al., ACM CSUR 2023 | Broad KT survey | No PRISMA Graph+SSL+strict-KC ontology |
| Shen et al., IEEE TLT 2024 | TLT KT models/variants/applications | Not a dedicated SSL-KT or provenance audit |
| Liu et al., NeurIPS 2022 pyKT | Deep-KT benchmark tool | Sequence-first empirical suite |
| Liu et al., IEEE TKDE 2025 | DLKT review + pyKT empirical study; DOI `10.1109/TKDE.2025.3552759` | Not an S-criterion SSL taxonomy or zero-exposure KC audit |
| Xu et al., JEDM 2026 | Dedicated Graph-KT structure/propagation survey | Not an SSL-KT survey; not a strict KC-cold-start construct audit |

**Wording used:** jointly synthesizes Graph-Based and Self-Supervised KT through a sparse-concept lens, with graph provenance, split control, strict cold-start construct validity, calibration, and reproducibility.

Xu pages 41–87 and the JEDM URL are taken from the official article page. No DOI was added because none was verified.
