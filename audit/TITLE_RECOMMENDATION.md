# Title recommendation

**Date:** 2026-11-25  
**Current title:** Graph-Based and Self-Supervised Knowledge Tracing in Sparse-Concept Settings: A Critical Survey

## Risk with the current title

“In Sparse-Concept Settings” can be read as claiming that the primary-core papers were *evaluated under* sparse-concept / zero-exposure KC protocols. Under the current freeze, strict KC cold-start remains \(0/44\). Sparse-concept is a **cross-cutting audit lens**, not the empirical setting of most included studies.

## Options considered

| Option | Title | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current | … in Sparse-Concept Settings: A Critical Survey | Matches existing portal/cover branding | Overstates evaluation setting |
| A | Graph-Based and Self-Supervised Knowledge Tracing: A Critical Survey of Structure, Sparsity, and Reliability | Accurate axes (provenance/SSL/sparsity/calibration) | Slightly generic |
| B | Graph-Based and Self-Supervised Knowledge Tracing: A Critical Survey Through the Sparse-Concept and Reliability Lens | Keeps sparse lens wording without claiming settings | Longer |

## Recommendation

**Prefer OPTION B** for the next manuscript version:

> Graph-Based and Self-Supervised Knowledge Tracing: A Critical Survey Through the Sparse-Concept and Reliability Lens

**Why:** Preserves the survey’s distinctive sparse/reliability contribution while avoiding the implication that included models were primarily tested in sparse-concept settings. Option A is acceptable if the editors prefer a shorter title.
