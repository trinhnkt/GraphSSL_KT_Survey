## 5. Evidence discipline

Never guess missing paper details.

Use:
- `NR` when relevant information is not reported;
- `NA` when a field is not applicable;
- `UNCLEAR` when evidence exists but cannot be coded confidently;
- `TBD_VERIFY` for unresolved metadata/factual verification.
